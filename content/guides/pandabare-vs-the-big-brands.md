## Questions to answer before you buy

- Does the stated size match your shoe size or wrist measurement?
- What are the full fibre percentages?
- How is the cuff and toe made, and is cushioning specified?
- How many socks or wristbands are included?
- What is the total with shipping, and what are the return conditions?

If a detail is missing, ask the seller. For a broader checklist, read our [Australian bamboo socks buying guide](/guides/bamboo-socks-australia-buyers-guide/).
